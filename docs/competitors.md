## C. Tutor reflection 2 — Find the gap

> Unlike a conventional news feed or a standalone fact-checking platform, GeoLens aims to combine historical background, geopolitical explanations, multiple perspectives and source-backed verification in one connected experience.

**Product hypothesis:** GeoLens would connect discovery → historical timeline → competing perspectives → evidence and claim status → follow-up explanation. These are **proposed features**, not a working, user-tested advantage. Ground News already supplies perspectives and context; Google News already groups multiple perspectives; Perplexity already provides AI research and citations [S1–S6]. A real user comparison is needed to discover whether GeoLens offers a meaningful improvement.
