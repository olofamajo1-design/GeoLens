- **Q6 — Uncertain claims:** “The claims of Hamas and IDF”
- **Q7 — What they did:** “The 1976 coup  in Nigeria. I want a movie that was speaking about it so i research more on youtube and google to hav a better understanding of it”
- **Q8 — Their perception:** “i notice bias in reporting from the western media”

These are P02's statements, **not independent verification** of any news claim or allegation of media bias.

## How this persona informs GeoLens (proposed, not validated)

P02's experiences are relevant to the idea of putting historical explanations, source-backed claims and differing perspectives in a single reader experience. GeoLens **aims** to reduce the need to move among scattered sources, but P02 **was not shown or asked to use GeoLens**. No measurable time saving, usage commitment, or willingness to pay has been established.

## Scope and limitations

The researcher's revised initial audience is **international-news followers seeking deeper understanding regardless of profession**, broader than the earlier student-and-young-adult hypothesis. P02 illustrates *one* person in that broader audience; P02 cannot represent all such readers. Five exploratory interviews support further investigation, not market-wide claims.

**Unknown and intentionally left blank:** specific occupation; exact age; typical day; news-consumption frequency; ranked top priority; actual cost of seeking background information; willingness to use or pay for GeoLens. A short follow-up with P02 would be needed to fill the tutor's “typical day” field with real observations.
