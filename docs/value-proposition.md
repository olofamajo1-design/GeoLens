# GeoLens — Assignment 3, Task 5: Value Proposition

## One-sentence value proposition (in the assignment's format)

For **Nigerian students and young adults who want to understand the background to global news and compare uncertain reports**, **GeoLens** is a **proposed AI-assisted news intelligence platform** that brings historical context, attributed evidence and multiple perspectives together; unlike a general news feed, it **aims** to connect explanation and source-backed verification in one place.

## Validated need versus proposed solution

**Interview evidence:** All five described social-media news discovery; interviewees provided examples of seeking historical context or confronting differing claims, and several described Google/YouTube/source-checking workarounds.

**Not validated:** GeoLens as an effective solution; superior accuracy, time savings, readiness to use, payment demand, or unique feature superiority. The product features above remain **proposals**.
