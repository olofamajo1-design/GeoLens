# GeoLens AI — Product Requirements Document

**Version:** 1.4 | **Purpose:** Assignment 4 — classroom validation PRD | **Date:** 24 November 2026  
**Tagline:** *Beyond the headlines. Understand the world.*  
**Source:** GeoLens Master PRD v1.1; five documented exploratory interviews. **Status:** Proposed validation plan, not commercial-launch approval.

## Executive Summary

**GeoLens AI** is a planned global news intelligence platform combining historical context, competing perspectives and verifiable sources, with strong Nigerian and African coverage. Its wider vision includes a human-supervised eight-agent newsroom, FactCheck and visual explainers. **This PRD scopes a classroom reader test**, without replacing the Master PRD.

## 1. Problem and Evidence

Some Nigerian international-news readers struggle to understand events' origins and conflicting reports, using multiple platforms for context.

In **five exploratory interviews**, all five participants mentioned social media as a news source, identified an event needing earlier context and gave an example of conflicting reports. **P01** searched Google after seeing Russia–Ukraine videos on TikTok; **P02** used YouTube and Google to research Nigeria's 1976 coup; **P03** searched Google after a report of drone strikes. These are reported past behaviors, **not proof of demand, time saved or payment intent**.

## 2. Target Users (and Who It Is Not For)

**Initial test group:** Nigerian students and working adults who follow world events and seek deeper explanation. **Evidence-based persona:** P02 is an Abuja-based adult aged 35+, who receives news through social-media recommendations and researches history online. Exact occupation, typical day and news-reading frequency were **not recorded**.

GeoLens aims for global reach later; this test excludes entertainment-only news and specialist trading.

## 3. Goals and Success Metrics (in Numbers)

**Goal:** Compare an integrated GeoLens explainer with participants' usual news-research methods (Google, YouTube or news websites). **Test case: Russia-Ukraine war.**

| Proposed measure | Target |
|---|---:|
| Real readers completing both approaches | **5** |
| Readers with a higher comprehension score using GeoLens (3 questions per approach) | **4/5** |
| Readers identifying at least one relevant source using GeoLens | **4/5** |
| Readers who research faster using GeoLens (minutes recorded for both approaches) | **4/5** |
| Readers preferring GeoLens for clarity/usefulness | **4/5** |
| Demo claims with attribution or explicit uncertainty | **100%** |

These are **proposed targets, not results**. Test responses and times are **TO CONFIRM**. With five users, findings will be exploratory, not statistically representative.

## 4. Value Proposition

For people following world news who need historical context and clarity about competing claims, **GeoLens AI** is a news intelligence platform connecting clear explanations, timelines and traceable evidence in one place. Unlike using separate news feeds and search services, it proposes one guided experience. **The advantage is untested.**

## 5. Features (Prioritized)

| Feature | Priority | Link to interview evidence | Effort |
|---|---|---|:---:|
| Quick Brief with dated historical timeline | **Must** | P01–P05 each wanted background to an event | M |
| Source links, claim attribution and uncertainty labels | **Must** | P01/P02/P03 researched or checked reports externally | M |
| Comparison of conflicting accounts | **Must** | P01–P05 each gave an example of conflicting reporting | M |
| Ask GeoLens contextual follow-up | Should | P02 researched further; the feature format is untested | L |
| Text/link FactCheck | Could | P03 searched to verify; a dedicated tool is untested | L |
| AI presenter videos and advanced forecasts | Not now | No direct demand shown in these interviews | L |

**Only three Musts** are in this test; other features remain on the roadmap.

## 6. MVP and User Journey

**Riskiest assumption:** One integrated explainer helps people understand the Russia-Ukraine war more clearly and efficiently than their usual fragmented research.

**MVP statement:** The MVP lets a world-news reader investigate one Russia-Ukraine war explainer so that we learn whether integrated context improves understanding.

| Step | User does | System does | Success looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opens the Russia-Ukraine case | Displays a sourced Quick Brief | Identifies the event |
| 2 | Reads the explanation | Presents key facts and uncertainty | Restates the main development |
| 3 | Opens historical timeline | Shows dated antecedents | Names relevant background |
| 4 | Compares differing accounts | Attributes claims to sources | Identifies agreement/disagreement |
| 5 | Checks supporting evidence | Opens traceable links | Recognizes evidence or gaps |
| 6 | Completes a short evaluation | Records consented answers and time | Researcher can compare outcomes |

**Validation method:** Five participants complete comparable, timed research tasks using GeoLens and their normal sources. Alternate which method is used first and use matched questions to limit practice effects. Record comprehension, source identification, time and preference. **No test has yet been completed.**

**MVP visual evidence:** See [MVP screenshots and evidence](mvp-screenshots.md) for the six planned prototype views (homepage, Quick Brief, historical timeline, conflicting accounts, source evidence and mobile layout). **Screenshots are TO CAPTURE** from the actual GeoLens prototype; they are not yet evidence of completed functionality. The separate five design-inspiration screenshots belong to Assignment 4 Task 5.

## 7. Out of Scope (Not Now)

**For this classroom test only:** live feeds, agent production, publishing, checkout and presenter videos are excluded—not canceled from the Master PRD. Advanced forecasting and forensics are later.

## 8. Risks and Assumptions

- **Actual demand — pending:** Follow-up questions were sent to previous interview participants. The number contacted, actual replies and verified commitments to a demonstration are **TO CONFIRM**; do not count invitations as commitments.
- **Benefit — pending:** Comparative Russia-Ukraine usability testing has not been conducted; test results, comprehension scores and time differences are **TO CONFIRM**.
- **Trust and rights:** Source conflicting claims, disclose uncertainty, use permitted material and keep consented feedback anonymous.
- **Editorial safety and feasibility:** Human approval is required before public release; live integrations, provider costs and source permissions still require verification.

**Decision:** Proceed with **limited validation only**; neither product adoption nor commercial launch is established.
