# GeoLens — Assignment 3, Task 1: Problem Statement

**Product:** GeoLens AI — *Beyond the headlines. Understand the world.*  
**Initial target group:** Nigerian university students and young adults who follow international news. This is a proposed initial segment, not a claim that all interviewees belong to it.

## Problem statement (in the instructor’s format)

Nigerian university students and young adults who follow international news struggle to **understand the background to major events and evaluate conflicting claims** because the news they encounter often provides incomplete context or differing accounts.

Today, some **search Google, YouTube or other news sources after discovering stories on social media**, which can involve extra research and leave questions about source trust unresolved. **Time costs and how often this happens remain unmeasured.**

## Four problem tests (1 = weak; 3 = strong)

| Test | Score | Why? (one line) |

| Frequent | 1 | Five people gave past examples, but none gave a measured frequency. |
| Painful | 2 | Respondents described confusion, uncertainty and difficulty assessing sources; no quantified time or financial cost. |

| Reachable | 2 | Five people were interviewed, but the target of reaching ten suitable users has not been demonstrated. |
| **Total** | **7 / 12** | **Qualitative judgment, not a validated market score.** |

## Interview support and important limits

- P01 searched Google after TikTok Russia–Ukraine videos.
- P02 researched Nigeria's 1976 coup on YouTube and Google.
- P03 searched Google to check a report about drone strikes.
- P04 reported checking both sides of a story and verifying its source.
- P05 reported checking coherence and consistency, but also said most news is understood through follow-up; it would be wrong to assume everyone struggles with comprehension.
- These five interviewees are a small convenience sample. P02/P03 ages are unknown and P05 demographics unknown; the primary target group is only partially represented.

**Status:** Research-informed problem hypothesis. Do not state that five interviews prove willingness to use or pay for GeoLens.
