# GeoLens — Assignment 3, Task 3: Interview Script

**Script length:** approximately 15 minutes. **Target:** readers of international news, initially Nigerian students and young adults.  
**Important:** P01–P03 were interviewed using the eight questions below. P04–P05 used a ten-question variation; do not pretend all five answered the same script.

## Part A — Rewrite the weak questions

| Weak question from the assignment | Better question about past behaviour |
|---|---|
| Do you think my idea would help you? | Tell me about the last time you struggled to understand an international news story. What happened? |
| Wouldn't it be great if there was an app for this? | The last time you needed background on a news story, where did you go and what did you find? |
| Would you pay for this? | Tell me about the last time, if any, you paid for news or research access. What made you do it? |

## Warm-up (1 minute)

“Hello, thank you for speaking with me. I'm taking an AI Product Development course and researching how people get and understand international news. I'm not selling anything. There are no right or wrong answers. This will take about 15 minutes. May I take anonymised notes?”

## Eight main questions (about 12 minutes)

1. How do you normally get information about what is happening around the world?
2. Can you tell me about the last international news story that caught your attention?
3. Think about the last time you read or watched a news story that you did not fully understand. What was the story, and what made it difficult to understand?
4. Can you remember a recent news story where you wanted to understand what happened before the event?
5. Tell me about the last time you came across different or conflicting reports about the same event.
6. Can you describe a time when you were unsure whether a news story or claim was true?
7. Think about the last time you searched different websites, videos or social media platforms to understand one news story. Walk me through what you did.
8. Looking back at your recent experiences following international news, what has been the most difficult or frustrating situation you have faced?

**Non-leading follow-up prompts:** “Tell me more.” “What did you do next?” “Where did you look?” “What was the result?” Ask only when appropriate.

## Closing (2 minutes)

“Thank you for sharing your experience. Are there two other people who follow international news and might be willing to share their experiences?”

**Ethics:** Ask before notes, use participant codes, omit identifying information, do not describe GeoLens during the interview.
