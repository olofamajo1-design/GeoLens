# GeoLens — Evidence Synthesis (Supporting File)

**Date:** 21 November 2026  
**Scope:** Five exploratory customer interviews, not proof of market demand.

## Evidence by participant

- **P01:** university student; social-media news; searched Google after TikTok Russia–Ukraine videos; asked about Iran background and differing End SARS accounts.
- **P02:** working professional/class; news surfaced via social-media algorithm; wanted Gaza/pre-October 7 context; used Google/YouTube for Nigeria's 1976 coup; perceived Western-media bias.
- **P03:** working class; social media; asked about US military installations and Soviet history; Googled reported drone strikes; concerned about trustworthy news channels.
- **P04:** student; news/social media; sought reasons behind US–Iran events; described conflicting celebrity/current-affairs stories and checking both sides; complained of double standards.
- **P05:** demographic details not documented; social media, news and radio; cited El Niño context; said generally understands news through follow-up, considers headlines/interest and uses source consistency to assess information.

## What could change the product plan?

Historical context and cited comparisons deserve user testing before a complex AI newsroom is built. Neither the interviews nor this document prove that users prefer GeoLens over Google News, Ground News or existing fact-checking platforms, or that anyone will pay.
